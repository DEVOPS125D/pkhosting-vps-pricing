Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about provisioning, billing, and server management.

How quickly is my PKHosting VPS provisioned after payment?

Orders paid via Raast, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or debit/credit card are provisioned automatically within 60 seconds. Your root SSH credentials and KVM console URL appear immediately in your client portal.

How does the annual billing discount work?

When you choose annual billing, you pay for 10 months and receive 12 full months of hosting—giving you 2 months free (16.67% off) locked in for your entire first billing cycle.

Can I upgrade from Starter or Growth without losing my data?

Yes. You can scale vCPU, RAM, and NVMe storage upward from your dashboard with a single reboot, preserving your IPv4 address, firewall rules, and existing disk volumes.

Do I get full root access and custom ISO support?

Every PKHosting VPS includes full root SSH access, a browser-based VNC/KVM rescue console, and one-click rebuilds for Ubuntu 24.04 LTS, Debian 12, AlmaLinux 9, or custom ISO images.

Which local Pakistani payment methods are supported?

We support instant Raast / IBFT bank transfers, JazzCash, Easypaisa, and Pakistani or international Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards with instant invoice verification.
